Aethvion

aethvion.com Steam Publisher


Aethvion is my independent game studio, based in Apeldoorn, Netherlands. We craft atmospheric horror experiences and precision games — all built on a shared in-house toolkit, so every title feels cohesive and ships faster.

As founder and solo developer I handle everything from design and programming to 3D art, audio and release, with the Aethvion toolkit (and the Aethvion Suite) doing the heavy lifting behind the scenes.

Games

* Rock Paper Scissors: The Final Match — horror RPS with online 1v1 and match modes
* Cannon Climbing — precision platformer with chained co-op
* Deep Sea Arena — underwater arena shooter
* Cornfield Escape — procedural cornfield horror
* Yukimura 幸村 — Japanese folklore survival horror
